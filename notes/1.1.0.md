- **Your bibliography beside the document.** Every entry in the paper's `.bib`, with author, year, title and venue, in file order or sorted by author, year, citation count or key.
- **What is actually cited.** Entries nothing in the text cites are marked, so a reading you dropped or a citation you meant to make is easy to spot.
- **Broken citations, before you print.** A citation that names an entry missing from the bibliography is shown right away, instead of as a "?" in the PDF.
- **Nothing hidden.** Anything in the `.bib` that could not be read is listed, rather than quietly left out.
